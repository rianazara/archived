Titles named as genuinely two-player rather than single-player games with a
second controller:

- **It Takes Two** — designed from the ground up for two, mechanically
  forcing cooperation
- **A Way Out** — split-screen throughout, built for exactly two
- **Split Fiction** — from the same designers as the above two
- **Unravel Two** — gentler, puzzle-platforming
- **Overcooked! 2** — chaotic, short sessions, reliably produces arguing
- **Baldur's Gate 3** — the long one; co-op across a full campaign

The framing: these aren't really about gameplay, they're about
communication, teamwork and working something out together — one minute
solving a puzzle, the next arguing or laughing or nearly ruining the run.

Useful selection note the post implies without stating: these span very
different commitment levels. Overcooked is an evening; Baldur's Gate is
months. Pick for the appetite you both actually have rather than the one you
aspire to.
