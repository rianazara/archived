The insight: regardless of technique — microblading, nanoblading, machine
hairstrokes — **the shaping is the same decision**, and it's the one that
matters. People arrive asking about technique and skip the conversation that
actually determines the outcome.

What to discuss instead: how you want to come across. Whether you want to
read as more delicate or more confident, bolder or more proper, or natural
and less controlled. An experienced artist adjusts the brow design to that
brief.

The warning attached is the reason this matters: brows change how you're
*perceived*, and well-executed brows in the wrong shape can read as
masculine or simply not like you. A technically flawless result in a shape
that doesn't suit your face is still a bad outcome, and semi-permanent means
living with it.

The transferable point for anything semi-permanent: have the conversation
about intent, not method. Method is the practitioner's domain; intent is the
only part you can specify and the only part they can't guess.
