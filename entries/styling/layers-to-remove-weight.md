The vocabulary for asking for movement without losing length:

- **Butterfly layers** — shorter internal layers over longer lengths, which
  creates volume through the top while the overall length stays. Grows out
  gracefully because the short pieces blend rather than sitting as a shelf.
- **Face-framing curtain layers** — the shortest pieces part around the
  face, softening the front without committing to a fringe.
- Styled with a **voluminous flicked blowout** for bounce and shine.

The stated purpose is "taking away heaviness while adding movement, shape
and structure," which is the useful way to phrase a request: heavy, flat
hair usually needs weight removed internally rather than inches taken off
the bottom.

Worth noting this was a salon's promotional post, and that layering
behaviour depends heavily on hair texture and density — fine hair can look
thinner when over-layered, which is the opposite of the intended effect.
