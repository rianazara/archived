Céline Dion — 200 million records, residencies, Grammys, Oscars — still
warms up every day, still works with a voice teacher, and demonstrated her
warm-up exercises on television because she still does them before every
show.

Since being diagnosed with **stiff-person syndrome** in 2022, a rare
neurological condition in which singing can trigger spasms in the larynx and
pharynx, she has continued vocal therapy five days a week.

The point drawn: the voice isn't a possession, it's something maintained,
protected and rebuilt — training rather than retreating. And the lesson
isn't aimed at beginners, since the person with the least to prove is the
one still warming up.

The transferable version beyond singing: capability that depends on a body
needs maintenance indefinitely, and the maintenance doesn't get optional
with seniority. Compare [[drills-for-becoming-articulate]] — the people who
sound effortless are generally the ones still practising.
