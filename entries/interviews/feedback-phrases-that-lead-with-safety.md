The diagnosis: managers who come across as harsh usually aren't being unkind
— they describe what went wrong without describing what good would look
like.

Phrases offered, with what each is doing:

- "I want to have a difficult conversation, and I want us to come out of it
  with a clear plan." — establishes safety and a destination up front.
- "The data is telling us X. Help me understand what's getting in the way."
  — puts the problem, not the person, in the middle.
- "If you were in my position, what would you be saying?" — builds
  perspective instead of arguing for it.
- "What do you think the three best solutions are, and which would you
  choose?" — hands over ownership of the fix.
- "What's getting in the way of you owning this?" — names the issue
  directly, without euphemism.

five carried over.
