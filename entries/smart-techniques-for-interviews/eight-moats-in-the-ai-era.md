Gokul Rajaram's framework (shared on 20VC) for which software companies
survive as software becomes cheap to build and replicate. Eight candidate
moats:

data · workflow · regulation · distribution · ecosystem · network ·
physical infrastructure · scale

The argument is that durability comes from holding several of these at once,
not one.

For early-stage companies he singles out two as mattering most:

- **Proprietary data that compounds with use** — it gets better precisely
  because customers use it.
- **Workflows embedded in how customers operate** — replacement means
  disrupting how the business runs.

The implied sequencing: build something hard to rip out before worrying
about the other six.
