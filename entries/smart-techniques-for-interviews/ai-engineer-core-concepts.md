A roadmap framed against collecting buzzwords:

1. **Prompt engineering** — getting consistent output from an LLM.
2. **RAG** — grounding answers in your own documents and data.
3. **Agents** — planning, tool calling, memory, autonomous execution.
4. **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** — how models connect to external tools,
   APIs and databases.
5. **Vector databases** — how embeddings are stored and searched.
6. **Fine-tuning** — and, importantly, when prompting or RAG is the better
   choice instead.
7. **Multimodal** — text, images, audio, PDFs, video together.
8. **AI workflows** — chaining prompts, tools, retrieval and business logic
   into something reliable.
9. **Deployment** — containers, APIs, cloud, monitoring, scaling.
10. **Evaluation** — measuring accuracy, latency, hallucination rate, cost
    and user experience instead of trusting intuition.

The framing worth keeping: the hiring demand is for people who can build AI
products, not people who can use a chatbot. Items 6 and 10 are the ones most
often skipped and most often what separates a demo from a system.
