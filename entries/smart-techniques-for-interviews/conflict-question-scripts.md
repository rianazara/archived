"I don't really have conflicts" reads as either low self-awareness or low
engagement, and "I let my manager deal with it" reads as abdication. The
question is checking whether you can stay calm, keep things factual, and
resolve without personalizing.

Angles that work, any one of which can carry the answer:

- Address disagreement early, and bring it back to shared goals.
- Listen first — understanding the other position usually shortens the path
  to a solution.
- Focus on root cause, then propose options rather than arriving with a
  verdict.
- Re-align on expectations, since a lot of "conflict" is just unclear
  ownership.
- Ask questions instead of reacting, which lowers the temperature.
- Bring in stakeholders early for alignment, framed as prevention rather
  than escalation.

Pair whichever you choose with a real example; the scripts are a structure,
not a substitute for having a story.
