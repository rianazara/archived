Naming a flaw is the easy half and not what's being assessed. The answer
that works has four parts: the real weakness, what you noticed about it,
what you changed, and how you handle it today.

What to avoid:

- **Confession with no resolution** — a flaw stated and left there.
- **A strength in disguise** — "I'm a perfectionist" is so worn it conveys
  nothing.
- **A weakness that disqualifies you** — pick something that doesn't
  undermine the core of the job.

The point isn't that hiring managers want candidates without weaknesses;
they want evidence of self-awareness, judgment and growth. The impression to
leave is that you know where you need to improve and you act on it.
