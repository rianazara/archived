From a former Amazon hiring manager: the question isn't measuring ambition.
It's testing four things — whether you think in scope or in titles, whether
you expand responsibility or wait to be promoted, whether you understand
business impact, and whether your trajectory aligns with the company's.

A weak answer names a title and "continuing to grow my skills." A
senior-level answer describes the level of ownership you're moving toward:
multi-team initiatives tied to revenue or efficiency, mentoring, influencing
cross-functional strategy — and ties that to where the organization is
heading.

Structure:

1. Anchor in business impact, not the title.
2. Show responsibility expanding — teams, budget, decision authority.
3. Align it with the company's direction, so your growth serves their
   strategy.
4. Stay specific and unhedged; no "I'm open to anything."

The distinction drawn was between an answer that signals hoping and one that
signals inevitability.
