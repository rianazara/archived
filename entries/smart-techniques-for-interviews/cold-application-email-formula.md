Four-part formula:

1. **Their problem, quoted from the job description.** Postings usually name
   the actual priority — reference that line specifically.
2. **How you solved that exact problem where you are now**, as a short list
   of what you did.
3. **The result in numbers.**
4. **One easy question** — typically "are you the right person to talk to
   about this, or can you point me to who is?"

The worked example cited a doubled customer-acquisition cost, listed four
concrete interventions (building a creator pipeline for ad volume, a
hook-testing framework with kill/scale thresholds, shifting reporting from
impressions to CAC and payback, bringing creative in-house), then closed on
CAC down 34% in a quarter.

Timing matters as much as the text: cold email works on freshly posted roles,
before hundreds of applicants. The approach was to find roles straight from
company career pages filtered to the last 24 hours, get the hiring manager's
name from the team page, and infer the email pattern.

Note the post named specific paid tools for those steps, which is also how
the creator monetizes — the formula stands on its own without them. Your
[[daily-pm-job-pipeline]] setup already covers the fresh-posting part.
