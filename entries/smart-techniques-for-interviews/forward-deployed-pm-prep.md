Three areas someone focused on while interviewing for forward-deployed
product roles:

- **Messy customer problems** — practice asking questions before designing
  anything: what the actual workflow is, what the AI should do, and where a
  human should step in. Also practice narrating a system design aloud and
  revising it when the interviewer adds a constraint.
- **Client-style case studies** — make a recommendation on incomplete
  information: state your assumptions and why, compare options on business
  impact, engineering cost, time to value and risk, then defend the choice
  and say what evidence would change your mind.
- **Behavioral** — real stories about using AI in your work, working
  directly with customers, and handling pushback, told in terms of the
  decisions and tradeoffs you made and what happened afterwards.

The most portable habit here is "what evidence would change my mind" — it
converts a recommendation from an opinion into a position you can hold under
pressure.
