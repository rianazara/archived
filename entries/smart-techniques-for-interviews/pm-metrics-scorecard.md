Asked how you'd measure the success of a newly launched product, "units
sold" measures the purchase, not whether lasting value was created.

Clarify the goal first — attracting new customers, driving upgrades, growing
a premium category, or driving adoption of one new feature are different
objectives with different metrics.

Then build three tiers rather than one number:

- **Business** — units, revenue and margin, preorders converted, upgrade and
  switcher rates.
- **Customer value** — devices still in active use after 90 days, adoption
  of the headline feature, satisfaction, pull-through into the wider
  ecosystem.
- **Guardrails** — returns, defects and repairs, battery complaints, app
  compatibility, support volume.

The point being tested is whether you can reason from the product's goal to
the metric, not whether you can recite a North Star framework.
