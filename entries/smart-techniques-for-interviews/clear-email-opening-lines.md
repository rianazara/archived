One opening line per situation, each putting the ask and the deadline where
the reader will actually see them:

| Situation | Opening |
|---|---|
| Need a decision | "I'm writing to get your approval on X by Thursday." |
| Following up | "Following up on X: could you confirm by Friday?" |
| Have a recommendation | "My recommendation is X, for two reasons." |
| Need to disagree | "I have a different view on X that I'd like us to consider." |
| Introducing an idea | "I see an opportunity to improve X." |
| Scope creep | "Our agreed scope covers X. This would add Y." |
| Cold outreach | "Your work on X caught my attention, particularly Y." |

The common thread: state what deserves attention and what you need, without
apologizing for being in someone's inbox. Warmth and having a position
aren't in tension — "I have a different view" is both.
