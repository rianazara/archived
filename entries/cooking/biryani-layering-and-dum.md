The structure matters more than any single quantity: build three things
separately, then let them finish together.

**1. Fried onions and fried chicken.** Fry sliced onions to golden, remove.
Marinate chicken (chilli powder, turmeric, salt, lemon, yogurt,
ginger-garlic paste) and fry. Keep the frying oil — it goes into the masala.

**2. The masala.** In ghee or the reserved oil, sauté crushed ginger, garlic
and green chillies. Add tomato and salt, cook soft. Add crushed mint and
coriander, then the fried onions, garam masala, lemon juice and yogurt.
Finally fold in the fried chicken.

**3. The rice.** Soak long-grain rice 30 minutes. Heat ghee with cardamom,
cinnamon and bay leaf, add the drained rice and sauté to dry off surface
moisture, then add hot water at **1.5 parts water to 1 part rice**, salt,
cover, cook low ~15 minutes.

**Dum.** Layer masala, rice, masala, then fried onions, raisins and cashews
on top. Cover tightly and cook on low ~10 minutes.

Two things carry the result: sautéing the soaked rice dry before the water
goes in, which keeps grains separate, and cooking the rice to just-done
since dum finishes it. The 1.5:1 ratio assumes soaked rice — unsoaked needs
more.
