Nippattu — the Karnataka rice-and-chana-dal cracker eaten with filter
coffee — is traditionally deep fried. Baking it cuts roughly half the
calories and, usefully, keeps the month-long shelf life intact.

The dough, scaled to ½ cup rice flour:

- 2 tbsp gram flour (besan)
- ½ tsp salt, ⅛ tsp turmeric, a pinch of asafoetida, ¼ tsp red chilli powder
- 2 tbsp curry leaves, 2 tbsp fresh coriander
- 2 tsp white + 1 tsp black sesame seeds, ½ tsp ajwain, ½ tsp jeera,
  1 tsp chilli flakes
- ¼ cup peanuts, 4 tbsp soaked chana dal
- **2.5 tbsp hot oil**, then 3–4 tbsp water

Press roughly between two sheets of parchment rather than rolling, and bake
until crisp.

Two things are load-bearing: the **hot oil rubbed into the flour** before
water, which is what makes the texture short and crumbly rather than hard,
and the **soaked chana dal** staying whole for bite. Press unevenly on
purpose — the thin patches crisp while thicker ones stay chewy.
