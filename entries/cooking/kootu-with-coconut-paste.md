A mixed vegetable poricha kootu, and a template for the whole family of
coconut-thickened South Indian dishes.

**Structure, in four moves:**

1. **Soak** equal parts toor dal and moong dal, 20 minutes.
2. **Pressure cook** the dals with cubed vegetables — eggplant, potato, raw
   plantain, cluster beans, carrot, tomato — plus 1 tsp turmeric and salt,
   water to cover. Six minutes in an Instant Pot, five whistles otherwise.
3. **Grind the masala** while that cooks: sauté 2 tbsp urad dal, 1 tbsp
   black peppercorns, 2 dried and 4 green chillies, 2 sprigs curry leaves
   and ½ cup grated coconut in coconut oil. Add 1 tsp cumin *off the heat*.
   Grind smooth with a little water.
4. **Combine and temper** — stir the paste into the cooked vegetables,
   simmer 2–3 minutes to a gentle boil, then finish with ghee, mustard
   seeds, urad dal, hing and curry leaves poured over.

The two dals do different jobs: moong breaks down into body, toor holds
some texture. Pepper rather than chilli powder is what makes it a *poricha*
kootu — the heat is aromatic rather than sharp.

Eaten as a side with rice, or mixed into hot rice with ghee.
