Across the high-protein recipes saved here, the same small set of
ingredients does the work:

| Base | Where it shines |
|---|---|
| Soya chunks | cheapest protein; crisp or mince ([[crispy-soya-chunks]]) |
| Low-fat paneer | savoury dishes *and* blended desserts |
| Tofu | cheela, scrambles — holds seasoning well |
| Besan / roasted besan | binder in kebabs, base for cheela |
| Chana dal, roasted chana | parathas, no-bake dessert bases |
| Hung curd / Greek yogurt | marinades, dressings, puddings |
| Oats | binder in kebabs, base for bowls |

Patterns worth noticing:

- **Blending paneer** turns it into a neutral high-protein cream — the trick
  behind the cheesecake and tiramisu versions
  ([[paneer-as-dessert-cream]]).
- **Combining two bases** is how recipes reach 35g+ per serving: paneer with
  hung curd, soya with paneer, tofu with besan.
- **Hung curd over regular yogurt** in marinades, because the lower water
  content means it clings instead of sliding off.

Real serving numbers from the saved recipes land at 18–37 g protein, with
the higher end always combining two bases rather than increasing one.
