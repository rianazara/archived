A repeating formula: **dates + something ground + cocoa**, blended to a
thick paste, pressed into a container, chilled, cut. No baking, no added
sugar or jaggery.

Three variants from the saved recipes:

- **Brownie bars** — 110 g roasted chana, 200 g soaked dates, 30 g cocoa,
  50 ml water. Press, top with melted dark chocolate and almonds, chill an
  hour, cut into four.
- **Seed brownies** — pumpkin, sunflower and sesame seeds with ~½ cup dates
  and ¼ cup cocoa. Same method; the seeds bring magnesium and fats.
- **Chia pudding** — 80 g dates mashed with boiling water, then peanut
  butter, Greek yogurt, chia, vanilla, salt, milk. Refrigerate overnight.

What makes it work: dates supply sweetness *and* the binding, so the
ground base can be almost anything with structure — roasted chana, seeds,
nuts, oats. Soak them if they aren't soft, or the paste stays gritty.

A pinch of salt in every version. Worth repeating that "no refined sugar"
isn't low calorie — the chana bars run ~380 kcal each.
