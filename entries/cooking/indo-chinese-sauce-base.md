Nearly every chilli/Manchurian-style dish in this collection runs on the
same premixed sauce, whisked cold before it goes anywhere near heat:

- **Salt and umami** — soy sauce (light for seasoning, a little dark for
  colour)
- **Heat** — green chilli sauce, red chilli sauce, or Schezwan chutney
- **Sweet and body** — tomato ketchup
- **Acid** — a splash of vinegar
- **Thickener** — ½ tsp cornstarch
- **Water** — to loosen it to pourable

Proportions vary by dish; one saved version runs roughly ½ cup ketchup to
¼ cup green chilli sauce, 2 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp Schezwan, 1 tsp vinegar and
50 ml water.

The order of cooking matters more than the ratios:

1. Sauté a lot of finely chopped garlic and ginger until fragrant.
2. Add onion, cook to lightly golden.
3. Add diced vegetables on high heat — they should lose the raw bite but
   keep crunch.
4. Add the fried protein, pour in the sauce, toss on high for ~2 minutes so
   it reduces and coats rather than pools.
5. Finish with coriander or spring onion off the heat.

Mixing the cornstarch into the cold sauce rather than adding it to the pan
is what prevents lumps.
