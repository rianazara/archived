Four saved builds, all landing 30–45 g protein, all under 20 minutes:

- **Pesto paneer toast** — toast bread, spread pesto, top with 150 g grated
  low-fat paneer mixed with red onion, coriander, chillies, bell pepper,
  oregano and salt. Add a little cheese and tomato slices, air fry 375°F for
  10–12 minutes until bubbling. ~40 g protein.
- **Tofu spinach corn cheela** — 100 g tofu, spinach, ½ cup corn, ½ cup
  besan, jeera, ajwain, salt, chilli, coriander powder, chaat masala, half a
  cheese cube. Cook like a pancake; serve with yogurt dip.
- **Apple pie oat bowl** — sauté 100 g apple with honey, cinnamon and a
  splash of water. Separately cook 50 g oats with 100 ml water, stir in a
  scoop of protein powder, top with the apple, Greek yogurt, a crushed
  biscuit and more cinnamon. ~45 g protein.
- **The daily yogurt bowl** — Greek yogurt, caramelised banana, chia,
  pumpkin/sunflower/flax seeds, walnuts, blueberries, honey.

The common structure: **a protein base + a cooked fruit or vegetable + a
crunch + an acid or spice**. Protein powder into cooked oats, and besan or
tofu into a batter, are the two easiest upgrades to anything you already
make.

Add protein powder *after* cooking the oats — it turns gluey if it boils.
