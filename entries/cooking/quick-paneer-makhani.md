The shortcut is replacing fresh aromatics with powders, which removes the
chopping and the long sauté. Serves 3, about 10 minutes.

- 2 tbsp butter, 1 cup tomato puree
- 1 tsp onion powder, ½ tsp each ginger and garlic powder
- ¼ tsp green cardamom powder, ¼ tsp mace powder
- 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tbsp honey, ½ cup water
- ¼ cup fresh cream, 1 tbsp fried cashew paste
- 200 g paneer, cubed; 1 tsp kasoori methi

Method: melt butter, cook the puree 2 minutes, add all the powders, then the
cashew paste. Stir in honey and water, simmer 2 minutes. Add cream off a
hard boil and mix until silky. Fold in paneer, 2 minutes. Crush the kasoori
methi between your palms and stir through at the end.

The three tips that matter:

- **Chilled cream, added late** — keeps it from splitting.
- **Crush kasoori methi in your palms** before adding; it releases far more
  aroma than dropping it in whole.
- **Lemon juice if the honey reads too sweet** — the sugar and acid balance
  is the whole dish.

Mace and green cardamom are doing the heavy lifting on aroma here, which is
what makes a powder-based version taste like it simmered.
