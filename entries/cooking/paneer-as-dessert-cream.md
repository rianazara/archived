Low-fat paneer blended smooth with a liquid behaves like a thick cream, and
carries 30g+ protein into desserts that normally have none.

**The base:** paneer + a little skimmed milk + a sweetener + vanilla + a
pinch of salt, blended until completely smooth. The salt and vanilla are
what stop it tasting like cheese.

Two builds from the saved recipes:

- **Peanut butter cheesecake** — 180 g paneer, 2 tbsp peanut butter, 1 tbsp
  honey, vanilla, salt, 150 ml skimmed milk. Pour into a container, top with
  melted dark chocolate and crushed peanuts, chill at least an hour.
- **Tiramisu** — 250 g paneer, ~4 tbsp honey, 150 ml skimmed milk, vanilla,
  salt. Layer with rusk dipped in strong coffee for 5–10 seconds only, dust
  with cocoa, chill 3–4 hours. **Don't freeze it** — the texture breaks.

Practical notes: blend longer than feels necessary, since any graininess
stays graininess. Dip the rusk briefly — it keeps absorbing after you move
on, and oversoaked rusk collapses into sludge.

Worth knowing these are calorie-dense despite the protein — the cheesecake
is ~600 kcal for the batch, not a free dessert.
