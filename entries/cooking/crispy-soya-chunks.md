The method that turns soya chunks from waterlogged to crisp, reused across
several saved recipes:

1. **Boil** in salted hot water until softened.
2. **Squeeze out all the water** — this is the step people skip, and it's why
   the result ends up spongy. Rinse and squeeze again if the soya smells raw.
3. **Season dry** — black pepper, red chilli powder, ginger-garlic paste.
4. **Toss in cornstarch** (around 2 tbsp for 60 g) until evenly coated.
5. **Air fry at 200°C for ~10 minutes**, flipping halfway, until lightly
   golden.

A lower-temperature variant (175°C, 10 min) appears in another recipe, with
the marinade carrying turmeric and a little cornflour instead.

Why it works: the squeeze removes water that would otherwise steam the
coating, and the starch layer dries into a shell. The same
squeeze-then-starch logic applies before pan-searing.

Typical macros for a 50–60 g dry portion built into a full dish land around
18 g protein per serving — soya is the cheapest protein base in this
collection.
