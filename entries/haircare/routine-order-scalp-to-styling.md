Product names date quickly; the sequence doesn't.

1. **Scalp first** — oil twice a week, left on overnight before wash day.
   Healthy hair is a scalp outcome.
2. **Cleanse** — shampoo chosen for what you want (volume versus bond
   repair are different jobs).
3. **Deep nourishment** — a mask on wash day, rich but not so heavy it
   flattens.
4. **Serum on damp hair**, immediately after washing. *Warm it between your
   palms first* — it spreads evenly instead of landing in one patch.
5. **Heat protection before any styling**, treated as non-negotiable,
   including for heatless methods where tension and overnight friction still
   stress the hair.
6. **Style** — heatless curlers for root volume, a wand when humidity would
   collapse them anyway.

The two transferable details: warming serum in the palms, and that heat
protection comes *before* styling rather than being something you remember
afterwards. Step 1 is the one most routines skip, and the one the rest
depends on.
