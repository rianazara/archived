Six commonly cited patterns, each needing a different approach:

1. **Hormonal** — DHT sensitivity, thyroid changes, falling estrogen,
   elevated cortisol.
2. **Metabolic** — insulin resistance affecting follicle health.
3. **Nutrient deficiency** — low ferritin, vitamin D, zinc, B12, or simply
   not enough protein.
4. **Scalp and gut** — inflammation and scalp conditions interfering with
   growth.
5. **Stress and poor recovery** — note the lag: increased shedding often
   appears **two to three months after** the stressor, which is why people
   misattribute the cause.
6. **Several at once** — offered as the reason "trying everything" didn't
   work.

The useful instruction is "test, don't guess": a supplement aimed at the
wrong cause does nothing, and ferritin, vitamin D, thyroid and B12 are
ordinary blood tests. The two-to-three-month lag is the single most
practically useful item here.

Caveats: this came from a fitness coach rather than a clinician, and the
"quick quiz" is lead generation for coaching. The post's own advice to see a
qualified medical professional for diagnosis is the right one — a
dermatologist for patterned loss, a doctor for the bloodwork.
