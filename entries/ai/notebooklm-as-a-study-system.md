Most use is "summarize this PDF," which is the least of it. Other uses worth
knowing:

- Turn recorded lectures into a study system — quizzes, flashcards, audio
  overviews.
- Work through technical documentation faster.
- Prepare for system design and technical interviews against real sources.
- Convert design docs and meeting recordings into decision-ready notes.
- Build mind maps for complex topics.
- Produce structured reports and research from your own source set.
- Generate audio or video overviews for passive review.
- Keep separate notebooks per project or subject so sources don't bleed.

The operating principle: give it the right sources, then use the tools on
*those*. The quality ceiling is set by what you put in, which is the
difference between this and asking a general assistant — it's grounded in a
corpus you chose.
