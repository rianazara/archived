The catch-22 is familiar: postings want experience, experience requires the
job. Ways around it:

- **Self-driven projects** — solve a real problem and document the process.
  The documentation is half the value, because it's what someone else can
  actually assess.
- **Hackathons** — build with a team under time pressure and come out with
  something showable.
- **Startups** — approach them directly and offer the specific skill you
  have.
- **Research assistantships** — contact professors working in your area.
- **Nonprofits** — volunteer the skill in tech, analytics, marketing or
  product.

The framing: you don't wait to be granted a first opportunity, you create
proof that you can do the work, and the proof becomes the portfolio.

Worth pairing with [[one-full-stack-portfolio-project]] — one documented
project that goes end to end outperforms five half-finished ones, and with
[[never-borrow-someone-elses-metrics]], since proof only counts if the
numbers are yours.
