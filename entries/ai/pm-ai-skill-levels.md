AI is becoming a baseline PM skill, but there are tiers to it:

1. **Prompting** — getting useful output reliably.
2. **Research** — using it to synthesize and move faster through discovery.
3. **Product design and prototyping** — mocking the thing instead of
   describing it.
4. **AI-assisted coding** — building a working prototype yourself.
5. **Building agents, workflows and internal tools** — automating the
   repetitive parts of the job.

You don't need to become an engineer. The advantage goes to PMs who can
research faster, prototype an idea, and automate their own busywork — which
compresses the loop between having an idea and showing it to someone.

The resume note attached: don't write "AI." Show what you can do with it,
which is the same point as [[ai-pm-resume-signals]] — a keyword is not
evidence.

Levels 3 and 4 are where the step change is, because that's where you stop
needing someone else's time to make an idea concrete.
