The failure mode named: people trying to break into product start by
collecting tools — Figma, SQL, Jira, Cursor, Amplitude, an assistant —
without deciding which job they want.

Roles that can sit on one team:

- **Product Manager** — decides which problems are worth solving.
- **AI Product Manager** — turns user problems into reliable AI products,
  then evaluates whether the AI works well enough.
- **Product Builder** — turns ideas into working prototypes (title varies
  most across companies).
- **Product Analyst** — uses data to understand behavior and measure
  outcomes.
- **Product Marketing Manager** — positioning, messaging, go-to-market.
- **Product Designer** — how the product works and feels.

Boundaries overlap and titles differ by company. Notably, an AI PM still
needs the core PM skills — strategy, discovery, prioritization — with AI
problem framing, model evaluation and system design as *additional* layers,
not replacements.

The advice: pick the work you want to own, then build the skills and the
proof for that role, rather than learning every tool.
