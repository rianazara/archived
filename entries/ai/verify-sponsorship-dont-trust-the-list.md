Several saved posts circulate lists of "50 companies that sponsor" —
startups, consultancies, semiconductor and biotech firms, finance and
retail. They're useful only as a shortlist of places to *investigate*, and
one of them was honest about why.

**The verification steps that matter:**

- Check the company's **recent** filings, not whether it ever sponsored.
  History decays, policies change, and a 2021 filing says little now.
- Confirm the **specific position** accepts the authorization you hold —
  sponsorship is per-role, and teams within one company differ.
- Verify the **current** policy rather than inferring it from a past hire.
- Use official employer databases for filings rather than a screenshot.

The strategy note is the same as everywhere else in job search: don't apply
everywhere. Build a targeted list, match your actual skills to the role, and
prioritize employers with both relevant hiring *and* relevant sponsorship
history.

Treat the broader genre with care — these posts are frequently engagement
bait with the real list behind a comment gate, and a list without the
verification work transfers risk to you.
