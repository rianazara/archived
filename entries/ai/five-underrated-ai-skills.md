- **API usage** — using an assistant through its web page is the new "knows
  how to Google"; the work is building AI into how teams operate.
- **Agentic workflows** — orchestration rather than one-off prompting:
  research, reporting and follow-ups running end to end.
- **MCP** — connecting models to actual tools and data (notes, mail,
  databases). Broadly supported, thinly understood.
- **Multi-agent systems** — designing the pipeline where one agent
  researches, another writes, another reviews.
- **Tool evaluation** — testing the crowded tool landscape and giving
  leadership a defensible answer.

The advice attached: don't chase all five, pick two and go deep for a few
months.

Treat the salary claims as marketing — the post gates its "full breakdown"
behind a comment, which is the business model. The skill ordering is still
sensible, and items 2–4 are exactly what [[agent-learning-order]] sequences
properly.
