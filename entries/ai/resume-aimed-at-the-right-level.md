The reframe: the resume often isn't weak, it's aimed wrong. An APM resume
and a VP resume shouldn't look alike, and neither should a FAANG and a
startup one.

What each audience is looking for as proof:

| Target | Proof it wants |
|---|---|
| Entry level | SQL, A/B testing, user research |
| Senior | roadmap ownership, strategy, revenue impact |
| Leadership | team building, P&L, executive communication |
| FAANG | scale, product sense, data rigor |
| Startup | 0-to-1, speed, closeness to the customer |

"Same title, different proof" is the line.

The practical consequence for tailoring: before rewriting bullets, decide
which column you're writing into, because the *same* accomplishment gets
framed differently — one audience wants the experiment design, another wants
the revenue number, another wants how fast you shipped it alone.
