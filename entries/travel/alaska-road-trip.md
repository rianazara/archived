A 14-day loop out of Anchorage, with the shape worth keeping even if the
stops change:

| Days | Where | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anchorage | Chugach State Park |
| 2–3 | Denali NP | Savage River, Mount Healy Overlook |
| 4–5 | Palmer & Hatcher Pass | Reed Lakes hike; helicopter to Knik Glacier |
| 6–7 | Kenai Fjords & Seward | boat tour, Harding Icefield trail |
| 8–9 | Homer | the spit, boat to Kachemak Bay State Park |
| 10 | fly-in park | Lake Clark or Katmai |
| 11 | Whittier | Byron Glacier or Portage Pass, ferry to Valdez |
| 12–13 | Valdez | Thompson Pass, fish hatchery |
| 14 | back to Anchorage | |

**Knik Glacier** gets its own mention from a separate saved post:
helicopter in, dry suits, paddleboarding among icebergs on glacial meltwater.

Why the route makes sense: it alternates long drives with multi-day bases,
uses a ferry to avoid retracing the Richardson Highway, and puts the one
fly-in park on a single dedicated day so weather cancellation costs you a
day rather than a leg.

Two things the itinerary doesn't say. Fly-in parks, helicopters and boat
tours are weather-dependent — build slack or expect to lose one. And the
expensive items here are the air and boat components, not the driving.
