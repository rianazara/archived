Non-hiking options around Rainier, useful when the group has mixed appetite
for elevation gain:

- **Kayaking at Alder Lake** — rentals from the Elbe Market, but you
  transport them yourself. Mornings are calmest.
- **Crystal Mountain scenic gondola** — panoramic views of Rainier and the
  Cascades on a clear day, with a restaurant and patio at the top. The
  cheapest way to get a high view without a climb.
- **Crystal Mountain adventure course** — suitable for older children and
  adults.
- **Cannibal hot tub** at Paradise Village Lodge — a tub heated over an open
  fire. Must be booked ahead.
- **Mount Rainier Scenic Railroad** — regular scenic runs plus themed ones
  (holiday, brunch).

The planning note that matters: the gondola is weather-contingent and
pointless in cloud, so keep it flexible rather than scheduling it on a fixed
day. Two of these require advance booking and one requires transporting
your own rental — all three are the kind of detail that sinks a day trip
when discovered on arrival.
