- **Stausee Steg, Liechtenstein** — the standout from a country that's
  mountains and castles and takes about half an hour to drive end to end.
- **Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina** — cobbled streets, the river, the old
  bridge. The advice is to **stay overnight**, since the town belongs to
  day-trippers in the middle of the day and is quiet in the morning.
- **Lago di Carezza, Italy** — small alpine lake, emerald water, the
  Dolomites reflected in it. About a five-minute walk from the car park;
  early or sunset for still water.
- **Eibsee, Germany** — intensely blue, ringed by mountains, swimmable, with
  a loop walk around it.
- **Douro Valley, Portugal** — terraced vineyards and river views; the
  slower entry on this list, and the wine is the point.
- **Lake Jasna, Slovenia** — man-made, which doesn't show; clear water
  under the Julian Alps.
- **Lauterbrunnen Valley, Switzerland** — cliff walls, 70+ waterfalls,
  villages in the floor of the valley.

The two transferable tips buried in here: **stay overnight in day-trip
towns**, and **go early or at sunset for reflections**, since still water
needs the air to be still too. Several of these are short walks from
parking rather than hikes, which makes them combinable on a driving route.
