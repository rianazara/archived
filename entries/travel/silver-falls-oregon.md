About 1.5 hours from Portland, best reached by car. The **Trail of Ten
Falls** is a ~7.2-mile loop taking 3–5 hours with stops, passing ten
waterfalls — and you can walk **behind** several of them, including South
Falls (177 ft, the iconic one) and Lower South Falls, which the post
flagged as the one people skip.

Practical notes worth having:

- **Spring and early summer** for the strongest flow; autumn for foliage
  and fewer people.
- **Good hiking shoes** — the trail gets muddy and genuinely slippery
  behind the falls.
- **A waterproof layer**, because you will get misted.
- **Arrive early** on weekends; parking fills and there's a day-use fee at
  the entrance.
- **Download maps** beforehand — patchy signal.
- Best photographs reported from the path behind South Falls, and early
  morning for softer light.

The walk-behind access is what distinguishes this from most waterfall
hikes, and it's also why the footing warning isn't boilerplate.
