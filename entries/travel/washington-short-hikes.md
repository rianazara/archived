- **Snow Lake** — roughly an hour from Seattle depending on traffic, and the
  classic answer when someone's visiting. Popular and deservedly so; the
  trick is to keep walking **past the first viewpoints toward the far end of
  the lake**, where the crowds thin out.
- **Snow and Bench Lakes**, Mount Rainier — off Stevens Canyon Road,
  2.5 miles round trip, about 610 ft of gain, two alpine lakes. Needs a
  park pass or America the Beautiful.
- **A Gifford Pinchot waterfall** — about 3.5 hours from Seattle, genuinely
  off the beaten path: expect rough forest roads, a trail that's hard to
  follow, and a few water crossings. Download the map before you go.

The recurring pattern across all of these and
[[north-cascades-hikes]]: **the crowd is concentrated at the trailhead and
the first viewpoint.** Distance is the cheapest solitude available — an
extra mile past where most people stop usually does it.

Second recurring theme: download offline maps. Signal is unreliable across
most of this terrain, and two of these specifically warn that the trail is
easy to lose.
