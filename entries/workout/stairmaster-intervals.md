| Minutes | Level | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | 3–4 | warm-up, easy pace |
| 5–10 | 5–6 | steady, controlled breathing, upright |
| 10–15 | 30s at 7–8 / 30s at 4–5 | intervals, repeat for five minutes |
| 15–20 | 5–6 | steady climb, find rhythm |
| 20–23 | 6–7 | push, still controlled |
| 23–25 | 3–4 | cool down |

A second saved version runs a similar shape in longer blocks (3 min easy,
5 min moderate, 3 min hard, 5 min moderate, 4 min, then cool down).

Two things both posts insist on:

- **Hands off the machine.** Leaning on the rails transfers your weight to
  your arms and quietly removes most of the work.
- **Cardio supports fat loss; it doesn't cause it.** One put it plainly:
  the StairMaster helps, nutrition decides the result. Pairs with
  [[sustainable-fat-loss-routine]].

Adjust levels to your own fitness and recovery — not every session needs to
be maximal, which is the point of having a progression rather than just
going hard.
