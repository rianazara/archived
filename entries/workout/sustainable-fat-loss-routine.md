Notable mostly for what it *doesn't* claim — no fat-burning drinks, no
starving, no carb elimination.

**Food.** Protein at every meal, plenty of vegetables, whole foods,
controlled portions. Cut sugary drinks, frequent takeout, mindless snacking
and heavily processed food. Explicitly: *add a protein source rather than
simply removing carbs.* And if you aren't hungry at dinner, don't eat
because it's dinner time.

**Movement.** 10–12k steps daily — being active across the day rather than
expecting one workout to carry it. Strength training 3–4× a week, full body,
with progressive overload, to hold muscle while losing fat. Then 20–30
minutes of cardio you actually enjoy.

**Recovery.** 7–9 hours of sleep, because hunger, energy and consistency all
depend on it. Water through the day.

**Measurement.** Weekly *average* weight rather than daily readings, plus
waist measurements, photos, energy and how clothes fit.

The framing worth keeping: the goal of the 30 days isn't maximum loss, it's
being consistent enough that a modest, sustainable loss becomes realistic.

Two honest notes: 4–5 kg in a month is at the aggressive end of
"sustainable" and depends heavily on starting point, and weekly averaging
exists because daily weight swings on water and food timing.

_General information, not medical or nutritional advice._
