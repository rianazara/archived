From someone describing what they actually keep up as a tired 35-year-old
parent — which makes it more useful than an optimal routine nobody
sustains:

- **Micellar water first thing in the morning**, before anything else; the
  one step kept unbroken for years.
- **Sunscreen daily, indoors included**, with tinted over plain for extra
  protection against pigmentation.
- **Tranexamic acid** continuously, for family-history melasma.
- **Vitamin C** most mornings, switching to gentler derivatives when
  breaking out.
- **A cica ampoule** straight after showering.
- **Body care as a floor, not a project** — lactic acid lotion after the
  shower, body sunscreen before going out.

The attitude is the actual content: skin doesn't have to be flawless, a new
spot isn't an emergency, and clinical treatments are effective but not
obligatory. A routine you keep beats a better one you abandon.

On the collagen drink mentioned — the post is honest that oral collagen is
contested, and the silica claim it makes alongside is weaker than stated.
Treat that one as optional; the sunscreen and tranexamic acid are the parts
doing the work. See [[pigmentation-needs-actives-and-spf]].
